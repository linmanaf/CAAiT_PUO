About CAAiT

Established August 2024 — Malaysia's foremost polytechnic AI innovation centre at POLYCC.

Ts. Marlina Abdul Manaf
HEAD, CAAiT @ POLYCC
Head's Message

"AI is not the future — it is the present."

CAAiT was established with a singular vision: to make Artificial Intelligence accessible, meaningful, and impactful for every student, lecturer, and community member within the TVET ecosystem. We believe that ethical AI, applied with purpose, can transform lives and redefine what's possible in polytechnic education.

From our first benchmarking visit to Melbourne in 2024, to international exchanges with Korea and Indonesia, and the approval of Malaysia's first Diploma in Applied AI — CAAiT is just getting started. We welcome collaboration, curiosity, and commitment from everyone who believes in the power of technology for good.

Our Identity

What is CAAiT?

"The Center for Applied Artificial Intelligence Technology (CAAiT) is a cutting-edge hub for AI innovation and development, dedicated to advancing AI technology through research, curriculum development, industry collaboration, and ethical implementation. It aims to drive progress, enhance human capabilities and transform TVET technological landscapes."

Our Mission

Four Pillars of CAAiT

Everything we do traces back to these four founding commitments.

Pillar 01
Elevate AI Platform
To elevate CAAiT as a prominent platform for AI enhancement across the polytechnic ecosystem.
Pillar 02
AI in Curriculum
To drive AI into the polytechnic curriculum and modernise the TVET pedagogical approach.
Pillar 03
Industry Collaboration
To collaborate with industries in the TVET landscape, bridging academic knowledge with real-world application.
Pillar 04
AI Certifications
To provide AI certifications recognised by industry leaders, enabling students to enter the workforce AI-ready.
Our Journey

CAAiT Overall Roadmap

Center for Applied Artificial Intelligence Technology — from establishment to future direction.

August 2024
Establishment of CAAiT
CAAiT was officially established at POLYCC in August 2024 — marking the beginning of applied AI integration in Malaysia's TVET polytechnic system.
Late 2024
Team Formation
Build multidisciplinary teams to explore AI trends, validate solution concepts, and drive collaborative innovation across departments.
2024–2025
Projects
Integrate AI-based solutions into industrial projects and curricula to align with institutional and industry needs, serving POLYCC stakeholders and the community.
2025
Focus Area
Pilot applied AI applications to evaluate performance, scalability, and real-world impact. Focus: talent development, knowledge sharing, curriculum development, and AI ethics.
2025–2026
Plans
Refine projects based on feedback, data insights, technology readiness, and the POLYCC Transformation Digital Plan — including the PAIR (POLYCC AI Reform Agenda).
2026 & Beyond
Future Direction
Expand CAAiT's innovations through commercialisation, collaboration, and sustainable AI ecosystem development. Impact: AI literacy & modern Professional Development Programme (PdP).
