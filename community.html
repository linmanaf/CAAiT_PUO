AI for All

AI for Everyone

New to AI? Here's what you need to know — no technical background required.

What is AI?
Artificial Intelligence is technology that enables computers to learn, reason, and make decisions — similar to how humans think. It powers everything from your phone's face unlock to medical diagnosis.
Why AI in TVET?
The workforce of tomorrow needs AI skills. TVET students who understand and apply AI will be better equipped for Industry 4.0 — making them more employable and innovative in their careers.
How to Get Involved
Join CAAiT workshops, attend our public events, follow us on social media, or reach out to collaborate. AI knowledge is for everyone — regardless of your background or experience level.
